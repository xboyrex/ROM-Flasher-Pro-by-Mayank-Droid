ROM FLASHER PRO V6 – FLASHING PROCESS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PART A — AOSP / CUSTOM ROM FLASHING (Fastboot + Sideload)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

① Open ROM Flasher Pro V6.

② Connect your device with USB Debugging enabled.

③ Click "Browse" and select your ROM ZIP.

④ Click "Reboot to Bootloader" (or manually boot into Fastboot Mode).

⑤ Wait until the device is detected.

⑥ Click "Start Auto Flash".

   ➜ The tool will automatically:
   • Extract the ROM ZIP.
   • Flash boot, init_boot, vendor_boot, dtbo and recovery images.
   • Reboot your device into Recovery automatically.

⑦ In Recovery:
   • Select "Format Data" and confirm.
   • Go to "Apply Update" → "ADB Sideload".

   ➜ After enabling ADB Sideload, the tool will automatically start sideloading the ROM.

⑧ Wait until the installation reaches 100%.

⑨ Once finished, select "Reboot System".

✅ First boot may take 5–10 minutes.
⚠️ Do not disconnect the USB cable or close the tool during the flashing process.

PART B — HYPEROS / MIUI FLASHING (MiFlash Official Method)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

① Open ROM Flasher Pro V6.

② Install all required drivers first:
   • Go to "Drivers & ADB Ready".
   • Click "Install All 5" → then "Verify in CMD".
   • Make sure all 5 WHQL drivers show as INSTALLED.

③ Connect your device with USB Debugging enabled.

④ Click "Browse" and select your HyperOS / MIUI ROM package:
   • Supported formats: .tgz (Fastboot ROM) or official MiFlash folder.
   • The tool will auto-detect the ROM type and switch to MiFlash workflow.

⑤ Put your device into Fastboot Mode:
   • Click "Reboot to Bootloader" OR
   • Manually hold Volume Down + Power until Fastboot logo appears.

⑥ Wait until the device is detected as Fastboot / MiFlash device.

⑦ Select your flash mode (MiFlash official modes):

   ➜ Clean All
      • Wipes all data + flashes the ROM (recommended for clean install).

   ➜ Save User Data
      • Keeps internal storage, wipes system partitions only.

   ➜ Clean All & Lock
      • Wipes everything and re-locks the bootloader (⚠️ irreversible).

   ➜ Qualcomm EDL 9008 Mode
      • For hard-bricked / dead devices (requires QDLoader 9008 driver).

⑧ Optional but recommended before flashing:
   • Enable "Validate md5sum.xml Before Flash" for integrity check.
   • Enable "ARB Guard" if your device has Anti-Rollback protection.
   • Click "Bootloader Info & Token" to verify device state.

⑨ Click "Start Auto Flash" (or "Open XiaoMiFlash GUI" for the official UI).

   ➜ The tool will automatically:
   • Validate md5 checksums (if enabled).
   • Parse rawprogram*.xml / patch*.xml files.
   • Flash all partitions via fh_loader / QSaharaServer.
   • Show live progress in the AUTOMATED PROGRESS panel.

⑩ Wait until the process reaches 100% and shows "Flash completed successfully".

⑪ Once finished:
   • For "Clean All" / "Save User Data" → device will auto-reboot.
   • For "Clean All & Lock" → bootloader will be re-locked automatically.

✅ First boot after HyperOS flash may take 5–10 minutes.
⚠️ Never disconnect the USB cable or close the tool during MiFlash flashing.
⚠️ Never interrupt EDL 9008 mode — it can brick the device permanently.
⚠️ "Clean All & Lock" will PERMANENTLY re-lock the bootloader — do not use unless you are sure.

PART C — KERNEL / RECOVERY FLASHING (AnyKernel3 / IMG)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

① Open ROM Flasher Pro V6.

② Connect your device with USB Debugging enabled.

③ Click "Browse" and select:
   • AnyKernel3 ZIP, OR
   • Single partition .img (boot / init_boot / vendor_boot / dtbo / recovery).

④ The tool will auto-detect the file type and switch to the correct workflow.

⑤ Select the target partition (auto-selected if detected from payload.bin).

⑥ Click "Start Auto Flash".

   ➜ The tool will automatically:
   • Flash the selected partition(s) via fastboot.
   • Verify the flash result.

⑦ Once finished, reboot to System or Recovery as required.

✅ For kernel flash: reboot normally after flash.
✅ For recovery flash: reboot directly to Recovery (do NOT boot to System first).
⚠️ Wrong partition selection can soft-brick the device — double-check before flashing.

GENERAL SAFETY RULES (ALL METHODS)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠️ Always use the correct ROM for your exact device model.
⚠️ Keep battery above 50% before flashing.
⚠️ Use a good quality USB cable and a stable USB port (prefer USB 2.0 for flashing).
⚠️ Do not disconnect the USB cable or close the tool during flashing.
⚠️ Do not run other ADB / Fastboot tools simultaneously.
⚠️ For EDL 9008 mode — never interrupt power or USB.
⚠️ "Clean All & Lock" and "Erase Userdata" are DESTRUCTIVE and IRREVERSIBLE.
✅ First boot after any flash may take 5–10 minutes — be patient.
✅ If the device gets stuck, wait 10 minutes before force-rebooting.
✅ Keep a backup of your data — flashing may wipe everything.
